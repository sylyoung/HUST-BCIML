# BFT

**Axis:** Transfer / adaptation strategy &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Paradigm:** MI &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Dataset:** BNCI2014001 (cross-subject LOSO)

**Stage configuration:** `EA · no-aug · EEGNet · Linear · BFT (test-time, no gradient)`

## Mechanism
Backpropagation-Free Transformations (the BFT-A variant) pass each trial through K label-preserving transforms at test time and aggregate the predictions with a temperature-sharpened weighted softmax, with no gradient step. It adapts by test-time-augmentation aggregation rather than by updating parameters.

## Provenance
- **Paper / source:** Li et al. 2026, arXiv:2601.07556 (BFT, Backpropagation-Free Transformations); BFT-A variant
- **Implementation:** Adapted from Li et al. 2026 (arXiv:2601.07556). DISCLOSED APPROXIMATION: the paper's learning-to-rank module is a ranking network r(.) plus a mapping network m(.) pre-trained on synthetic data with an L1 rank loss, following SoDeep differentiable sorting; the SoDeep sorter weights are not released. Here r(.) is the authors' EEGNetLossPredictor (a 3-layer MLP on the flattened backbone features) trained on the source with a soft-rank Spearman surrogate over the K test-time views, and the aggregation weight per view is softmax(-predicted_loss) accumulated as a running mean over the stream. m(.) is not instantiated, because its output does not enter the aggregation. The backprop-free multi-transform structure, the predictor architecture, its inputs and its rank target are the authors'; the rank objective is a surrogate. The aggregation temperature is tau = 0.25 (config `bft_temp`); the paper uses 0.5.
